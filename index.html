    Race facts

    Date: Sunday, Nov 14, 2027 (confirmed, ironman.com)
    Start: ~7:20 AM CT rolling start (expected)
    Distance: 70.3 mi — 1.2 swim · 56 bike · 13.1 run
    Village / finish: Indian Spring Park · Suspension Bridge
    Transition (2026): Heritage Square, 311 Austin Ave
    Swim start (2026): Pecan Bottom Park, ~1 mi walk from T
    Swim: 1 lap, downriver in the Brazos River
    Bike: 1 loop south of Waco · ~1,035 ft gain
    Run: 2 loops, Brazos riverwalk & Cameron Park · ~299 ft gain
    Registration: open · ~$487.63 individual · Flex90 eligible · not registered

    Cutoffs (2026 Athlete Guide)

    Swim1:10from your own start
    Swim + T1 + bike5:30from your own start
    Bike checkpoint1:10 PM CTAid Station 3 (FM 434 & S 3rd St)
    Run checkpoint3:30 PM CTRiverwalk under Washington Ave Bridge
    Overall8:30finish line

    2027 cutoffs will be confirmed in the 2027 Athlete Guide.

    Weather expectations (mid-Nov)

    • Normal for Nov 14: high ~69°F / low ~46°F (NWS Waco climate normals)
    • IRONMAN race profile: high 79°F · low 54°F · water ~65°F
    • Records for Nov 14: 91°F (1989) and 22°F (2018), so expect anything from warm to a windy cold front
    • Wetsuit: legal at ≤76.1°F water, so a cool November swim is likely wetsuit-legal (the Oct 2024–25 races were 81°F and not wetsuit-legal)
    • Note: the 2026 swim (Oct 4) was canceled on race day

    The race moved from early October to mid-November for 2027, which matches the “cooler is better” preference.

    Course overview

    2.4 mi downriver swim, a fast, flat 112 mi bike through farm and wine country, and a 26.2 mi run finishing at the State Capitol. IRONMAN and Visit Sacramento's host agreement runs through 2028. Official page: ironman.com/races/im-california

    IRONMAN California reference maps are from 2025–26 (swim/bike 2026, run 2025) and may change before 2028.
